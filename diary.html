The First day nerves

Walking into campus not knowing anyone, overthinking everything—what to wear, where to sit, how to act. Wandering around buildings like you know where you’re going… but actually checking your phone every 2 minutes.

The Reality hits

When you realized college isn’t like high school—deadlines, professors, independence. Figuring things out on your own—commuting, managing time, surviving a whole day of classes. But somewhere in between the stress, the awkward silences, and the long days, things started to change. I found people who made me laugh, moments that made me feel seen, and little reasons to keep going.

The “everything felt new” atmosphere

Every day felt like stepping into a place that wasn’t mine yet. The classrooms were bigger, the hallways louder, and the faces around me were all strangers living their own stories. Slowly, unfamiliar became routine. The once-confusing paths became familiar walks. The strangers became classmates, then friends. The fear didn’t disappear completely, but it softened.

The “first breakdown / first comeback”

So far, that was my first real breakdown. The people I treated as friends—like family—ended up betraying me over something so small. But in the middle of that, I found my comeback: I met people who are genuinely kind, fun, and who truly care about each other
